Coach Fingerprints

Each tactical claim is turned into a number, and each coach's team is ranked against the teams it can fairly be compared with. A percentile of 90 means the team does more of that thing than 90% of its comparison group.

Do the claims hold up?

Verdicts use the average percentile of the metrics behind each claim. 67 or higher counts as supported, 33 or lower as not supported.
